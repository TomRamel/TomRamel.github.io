Checkmate Mate

Privacy Policy — Checkmate Mate

Last updated: 12 November 2026. Version française

Checkmate Mate is an Android app for analysing chess games. It works without an account. The publisher runs no server: it does not receive, see or keep any of your data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your games, their analyses, your notes, statistics, usernames and settings are stored only inside the app, on your device.
  • A backup or an export happens only when you ask for it, in the folder you choose.
  • If Android backup is enabled for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in it.
  • If you use AI interpretation, your access key is encrypted on the device and is never included in the app's backups.

What leaves your phone

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To do so, Google collects and processes the device's advertising ID, information about the device and the app, the IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), your interactions with ads and diagnostic data.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, your consent is requested at first launch. You can change it at any time in Settings → Technical → Ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Privacy → Ads).
  • Details: How Google uses data and Google Privacy Policy.

Lichess and Chess.com

When you import games (by username or by link), or when the app queries endgame tablebases, it contacts Lichess or Chess.com directly. The request contains the username, link or position concerned; these services see your IP address, like any website.

AI interpretation (optional, off by default)

If you turn this feature on, you choose an AI provider yourself (for example Google Gemini, OpenAI, Mistral, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Groq or your own server) and you supply your own key. With each request, the app sends that provider the data of the analysed game (moves, positions, evaluations, statistics) and your key. This processing is governed by the terms of the provider you chose. The texts are generated by an AI and may contain errors.

Reporting an AI text

The report button opens your email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent without your action.

Permissions requested

  • Internet: ads, game import, endgame tablebases, optional AI.
  • Notifications: progress of batch analysis.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: keep a batch analysis running while the screen is off.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

All of the app's data is on your device: you can delete it from the Library, with "Clear data" in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. The publisher holds nothing to delete. For data processed by Google, Lichess, Chess.com or your AI provider, please contact them.
